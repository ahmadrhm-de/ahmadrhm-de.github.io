English — Privacy Policy

1. Summary

  • No cross-app tracking / no ATT prompt (IDFA zeroed on iOS): Jumpy Alien does not include Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt (NSUserTrackingUsageDescription is omitted from Info.plist and InfoPlist.strings), so iOS returns a zeroed IDFA (00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000) and the app performs no developer-initiated cross-app tracking.
  • No user accounts or cloud profiling: All gameplay progress, high scores, unlocked aliens/trails, and settings remain locally on your device.
  • Non-personalized ads only (with regional UMP consent & Limited Ads support): Ad-supported runs and optional rewarded videos request only non-personalized ads via Google Mobile Ads (AdRequest(nonPersonalizedAds: true)). Where legally required (e.g., EEA/UK/Switzerland), Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents a consent dialog before ad initialization, and you can review or revoke your choice anytime under Settings → Privacy Settings / Datenschutz.

2. Information Processed

A. Local Game Data (Stored on Your Device)

  • Game progress (high scores per mode/difficulty, best planet reached per mode/difficulty, daily challenge & mission state, settings/language) stored locally via iOS preferences (UserDefaults / SharedPreferences).
  • Local virtual economy ledger (coins, revive crystals, unlocked skins/trails, permanent entitlements, verified StoreKit transaction IDs, and a random per-install install_uuid / appAccountToken excluded from iCloud/iTunes backups via NoBackup) stored in environment-isolated local SQLite databases (jumpy_alien_release_sandbox.db / jumpy_alien_release_production.db).
  • Deleting the app removes local preferences and the local SQLite database (durable Apple ID entitlements can be restored via Restore Purchases).

B. Advertising Data (Google Mobile Ads / UMP)

  • Non-personalized ads only: Every Google Mobile Ads request is configured with nonPersonalizedAds: true and child-appropriate MaxAdContentRating.g.
  • Regional consent & Limited Ads (Google UMP): In supported jurisdictions, Google UMP collects and stores consent choices. Changing or revoking consent in Settings → Privacy Settings / Datenschutz immediately disposes all preloaded ads (_interstitial and _rewarded), invalidates any in-flight ad load callbacks from the prior consent epoch so stale ads cannot be cached or shown, and either disables ad requests (canRequestAds == false) or reloads fresh ads matching the updated consent state (such as Limited Ads when canRequestAds == true).
  • SDK-declared signals & Privacy Manifest disclosures: Although Jumpy Alien omits NSUserTrackingUsageDescription (so iOS returns a zeroed IDFA) and sends nonPersonalizedAds: true, the embedded GoogleMobileAds.framework privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) declares SDK capabilities for Device ID (NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypeDeviceID, marked Linked & Tracking at the SDK capability level, e.g., non-IDFA/app-scoped device identifiers or SDK fraud/frequency-capping signals), Coarse Location (IP-derived region), Advertising Data, Product Interaction, Performance Data, Crash Data, and Other Diagnostic Data; UserMessagingPlatform.framework additionally declares Coarse Location, Performance Data, and Product Interaction for consent functionality. Under Limited Ads / non-personalized delivery, processing is restricted to contextual delivery, regional compliance, frequency capping, aggregated reporting, and fraud prevention.
  • Zero interstitials after purchasing Remove Ads / Starter Pack: Rewarded ads remain strictly opt-in (revive or bonus run coins) and are only offered when an ad is preloaded and eligible.

C. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit 2)

  • All payments are processed directly by Apple StoreKit 2 using your Apple ID.
  • Permanent / Non-Consumable perks (remove_ads, double_coins, and durable perks of starter_bundle — Mecha-Alien skin & permanent Remove Ads) are linked to your Apple ID and restorable across devices or reinstallations via Restore Purchases (AppStore.sync()). Entitlements are reconciled automatically on launch and app resume, including refunds/revocations.
  • Consumables (coins_small, coins_medium, coins_large, plus one-time Starter Pack 3,000 coins & 5 revive crystals) are credited locally on the active installation. A backup-excluded per-install UUID (appAccountToken) is attached to StoreKit purchases where supported to correlate uncredited transactions safely on that installation.
  • We never see or store your credit card, billing address, or Apple ID password.

3. Children's & Family Considerations

Jumpy Alien requests family-friendly ads (MaxAdContentRating.g), does not use behavioral profiling or IDFA tracking, and does not collect personal profiles, names, emails, or precise GPS location data.

4. Your Rights & Choices

  1. Review or revoke ad consent anytime: Open Settings → Privacy Settings / Datenschutz to reopen the Google UMP privacy options form.
  2. Privacy Policy link, summary & ad reporting in-app: Open Settings for the direct Privacy Policy (Online) link, the built-in summary, and Report Inappropriate Ad guidance (plus AdChoices inside ad overlays).
  3. Remove non-optional interstitials: Purchasing Remove Ads or the Cosmic Starter Pack permanently disables interstitial ads.
  4. Restore or support for IAPs: Use Restore Purchases for Apple ID-linked non-consumables, or contact support with your Apple receipt (reportaproblem.apple.com) and the installation ID shown in Settings.

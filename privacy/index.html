2. Information Processed, Legal Bases & Device Storage (§ 25 TDDDG / Art. 6 GDPR)

A. Local Game Data & Strictly Necessary Device Storage (§ 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)

  • Game progress (high scores per mode/difficulty, best planet reached per mode/difficulty, daily challenge & mission state, settings/language) stored locally via iOS preferences (UserDefaults / SharedPreferences).
  • Local virtual economy ledger (coins, revive crystals, unlocked skins/trails, permanent entitlements, verified StoreKit transaction IDs, and a random per-install install_uuid / appAccountToken excluded from iCloud/iTunes backups via NoBackup) stored in environment-isolated local SQLite databases (jumpy_alien_release_sandbox.db / jumpy_alien_release_production.db).
  • Local storage of your UMP consent status so the app remembers your privacy choice.
  • § 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG: Accessing and storing these local game, preference, consent, and purchase-ledger files on your device is strictly necessary to provide the game features and in-app purchase fulfillment you explicitly requested and therefore does not require separate device-storage consent. Deleting the app removes local preferences and the local SQLite database (durable Apple ID entitlements can be restored via Restore Purchases).

B. Advertising & Consent Management (Google Mobile Ads / UMP — § 25 Abs. 1 TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(a) & Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

  • § 25 Abs. 1 TDDDG & Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (Standard Non-Personalized Ads): Accessing or storing non-strictly-necessary advertising identifiers/local storage on your device (e.g., for frequency capping, aggregated reporting, and ad-delivery measurement) occurs in the EEA/UK/Switzerland only after your prior informed consent via Google UMP.
  • Non-personalized ads vs. Limited Ads (AdRequest(nonPersonalizedAds: true) & UMP): Every Google Mobile Ads request sets nonPersonalizedAds: true and child-appropriate MaxAdContentRating.g (no behavioral profiling). Standard Non-Personalized Ads (NPA) (when required regional storage consent is granted) use contextual signals and local storage/identifiers for frequency capping, aggregated reporting, and fraud prevention. When a user declines local storage in EEA/UK/Switzerland and UMP permits Limited Ads (canRequestAds == true), Google Mobile Ads accesses no local identifiers or cookies (and performs no SDK-side frequency capping), operating strictly under contextual delivery and security/fraud-prevention signals based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in ad-funded app operation and invalid-traffic/fraud defense).
  • Regional consent & post-timeout reconciliation (Google UMP): In supported jurisdictions, Google UMP collects and stores consent choices at startup and under Settings → Privacy Settings / Datenschutz. Completing, updating, or revoking consent in the initial UMP dialog (even if dismissed after the 20-second startup timeout) or in Settings → Privacy Settings / Datenschutz immediately disposes all preloaded ads (_interstitial and _rewarded), invalidates any in-flight ad load callbacks from the prior consent epoch so stale ads cannot be cached or shown, and either disables ad requests (canRequestAds == false) or reloads fresh ads matching the updated consent state.
  • SDK-declared signals & Privacy Manifest disclosures: Although Jumpy Alien omits NSUserTrackingUsageDescription (so iOS returns a zeroed IDFA) and sends nonPersonalizedAds: true, the embedded Google SDK privacy manifests — GoogleMobileAdsResources.bundle/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy (GoogleMobileAds.framework 12.14.0) and UserMessagingPlatformResources.bundle/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy (UserMessagingPlatform.framework 3.1.0) — declare SDK-level capabilities including Device ID (NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypeDeviceID, marked Linked & Tracking at Google's SDK manifest level), Coarse Location (IP-derived region), Advertising Data, Product Interaction, Performance Data, Crash Data, and Other Diagnostic Data. Note that omitting ATT alone does not override SDK-level manifest declarations.
  • Zero interstitials after purchasing Remove Ads / Starter Pack: Rewarded ads remain strictly opt-in (revive or bonus run coins) and are only offered when an ad is preloaded and eligible.

C. In-App Purchases & Statutory Withdrawal Notice (Apple StoreKit 2 — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)

  • All payments are processed directly by Apple Distribution International Ltd. / Apple Inc. via Apple StoreKit 2 using your Apple ID (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Entitlements are reconciled automatically on launch and app resume, including refunds/revocations.

A backup-excluded per-install UUID (appAccountToken) is attached to StoreKit purchases where supported to correlate uncredited transactions safely on that installation.

  • Statutory Withdrawal & Refunds (§ 356 Abs. 5 BGB / Apple App Store): Digital in-app content is delivered immediately upon purchase confirmation. Under EU/German consumer law (§ 356 Abs. 5 BGB), the statutory right of withdrawal for digital content expires once performance begins after your express consent and acknowledgment of expiry, subject to Apple's Media Services Terms & Conditions. Billing inquiries and refund requests are handled directly by Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

Contact Support / Support kontaktieren

Need help with Jumpy Alien (iPhone & iPad)? Send an email to arstudio.dev1@gmail.com. If your inquiry concerns an In-App Purchase, please include your Apple receipt (from reportaproblem.apple.com) and the Installation ID shown in Settings.

Du brauchst Hilfe zu Jumpy Alien? Schreib uns an arstudio.dev1@gmail.com — bei Fragen zu In-App-Käufen gerne mit deinem Apple-Beleg (reportaproblem.apple.com) und der in den Einstellungen angezeigten Installations-ID.

Purchases & Restore / Käufe & Wiederherstellung

  • Permanent perks (Non-Consumables): Remove Ads, 2x Coin Doubler, and the durable perks of the Cosmic Starter Pack (Mecha Titan skin + permanent Remove Ads) are linked to your Apple ID and can be restored anytime on another device or after reinstalling via Settings → Restore Purchases (or in the Shop).
  • Consumables: Coin packs and the one-time coins/crystals bonus inside the Starter Pack apply to the active installation.
  • Refunds: Refunds are processed by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy & Ad Settings / Datenschutz & Werbung

  • In EEA/UK/Switzerland you can review or withdraw ad consent anytime inside the app under Settings → Privacy Settings / Datenschutz.
  • To report an inappropriate ad, open Settings → Report Inappropriate Ad or use the AdChoices button inside the ad overlay.
